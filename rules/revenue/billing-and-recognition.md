# Billing and Recognition

Revenue is recognised when it is earned and billed when it is consumed. Between
the two sits the reconciliation that catches every error before it reaches a
finance statement.

## Recognition

- **No revenue before delivery or acceptance.** A signed contract is not revenue;
  it is an obligation.
- **No pull-forward or push-out without documented justification and finance
  approval.** Timing is the easiest number to lie with and the hardest to unwind.
- **The obligation is tied to a deliverable with an owner.** A contract with no
  delivery evidence is deferred, not earned.
- **Non-standard terms change the schedule, not the total.** A term that spreads
  recognition is a decision to record, never to estimate.

## Billing

- **Packaging and entitlements are accurate in billing.** A customer paying for a
  tier they do not have, or using one they did not buy, is a product
  configuration defect — not a billing quirk to be tolerated.
- **No unbilled usage.** Metered usage that never reaches an invoice is revenue
  silently given away, and it only surfaces at renewal.
- **No overbilling.** The same defect in the other direction becomes a refund, a
  chargeback, and a lost reference.
- **Monthly reconciliation across CRM, quoting, and billing**, with every
  difference explained rather than netted out. A netted variance is a hidden error
  with a plausible total.

## Escalation

- **A reconciliation break is reported with the amount, the account, and the
  likely cause** — not silently corrected, and never backdated without a record.
- **A customer-visible billing error is disclosed to the customer**, with the
  correction and the credit, before they discover it themselves.
