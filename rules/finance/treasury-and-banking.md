# Treasury and Banking

Treasury is where a single wrong value moves money irreversibly. The controls here
exist because a payment, once sent, is a negotiation to recover.

## Bank details

- **A bank account change requires an out-of-band call-back and dual approval.**
  Out of band means a number from the contract or the vendor's own website — never
  the number in the email that requested the change.
- **Treat a change of bank details as the highest-risk instruction in finance.** It
  is the entire mechanism of business email compromise, and it arrives looking
  routine.
- **Verify on first registration and on every subsequent change.** A verified vendor
  last year is not a verified account today.
- **A rushed payment is a reason to slow down.** Urgency is the pressure an attacker
  applies, so it is evidence, not a justification.

## Cash

- **A rolling cash-flow forecast is maintained and dated.** A cash position without a
  horizon is a fact that expires in a week.
- **The liquidity buffer is stated in policy and measured against it.** A buffer
  nobody measures is an intention.
- **No unauthorized borrowing or hedging.** A facility, an overdraft, or a forward
  contract is a decision with a signatory, not an operational convenience.
- **No speculative trading with company funds.** Treasury exists to protect the
  business's cash, not to take a position on the market.

## Payments

- **Payment files are generated by the system, reviewed, and released by someone who
  did not create them** — the segregation of duties in `controls-and-approvals`.
- **Payments run on a schedule, not on demand.** An exception payment is an approval
  with a name on it.
- **Reconcile the payment run to the bank statement the same day.** A payment
  assumed to have cleared is a payment that may still be a fraud in progress.
