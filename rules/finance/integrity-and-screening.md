# Integrity and Screening

Two questions decide whether a payment is commercial or criminal: **who is on the
other side**, and **what did they get beyond the goods**.

## Counterparties

- **Sanctions and PEP screening runs before onboarding** a vendor, customer, or
  partner — and again when ownership changes. A clean screen at onboarding is a
  photograph, not a control.
- **Screen the beneficial owner, not just the entity.** A sanctioned individual behind
  a clean shell company is the normal shape of the problem.
- **A hit is escalated, never self-cleared.** The decision belongs to compliance, and
  a payment made while "looking into it" is already a breach.
- **Screening applies to resellers and agents too.** An intermediary is where a
  prohibited payment is laundered into a plausible one.

## Gifts and facilitation

- **Facilitation payments are prohibited outright.** A payment to speed up a routine
  act is a bribe with a friendlier name and identical legal exposure, and it is the
  one form of corruption most likely to be treated internally as normal. The
  catalogue of prohibited inducements is in `sales-conduct`; here the point is that
  it applies to every counterparty, not only to buyers.
- **No gift beyond the policy threshold**, and no gift to a government official through
  a third party. Routing it through an agent does not change who received it.
- **Every gift and hospitality item is recorded in the register** — recipient,
  organisation, value, date, and reason — above the threshold, and the register is
  reviewed, not merely kept.
- **The buyer-facing rule is `sales-conduct`.** This rule owns the register and the
  screening, because the obligations run wider than the sales team.

## Raising a concern

- **A whistleblower channel exists, is reachable, and allows anonymity.** A channel
  that requires a name is a channel nobody uses.
- **No retaliation, and the policy says so in writing.** Retaliation is the reason
  channels go quiet, and it is a separate offence.
- **A reported concern is triaged by someone outside the line it implicates.** A
  manager investigating their own report is a decision to find nothing.
- **Outcomes are recorded, including unsubstantiated ones.** A register of concerns
  that only shows confirmed findings is a register of the past, not of the process.
