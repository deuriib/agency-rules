# Claims and Evidence

A marketing claim is a promise the business must be able to defend with a
document, years later, to a regulator. If the evidence is not on file when the
claim is written, the claim is not ready.

## Substantiation

- **Substantiation exists before publication**: the study, the test report, the
  benchmark, the signed statement. A claim you cannot document is a claim you
  cannot make, and "everyone knows" documents nothing.
- **No false, misleading, or unsubstantiated claim — including by omission.**
  "Up to 3× faster" without the conditions that produced the 3× is misleading,
  not vivid.
- **The qualifier sits next to the claim**, not in a footnote nobody reads and
  not only in the terms.
- **Numbers are reproducible.** State the sample, the period, and the baseline. A
  percentage without a baseline is decoration.
- **Claims expire with their evidence.** When the study ages out or the product
  changes, the claim goes with it.

## Comparison

- **Comparative advertising needs verified, current, like-for-like data**, and
  legal review before it runs.
- **Name the basis of comparison.** Same metric, same period, same conditions —
  or say that they differ.
- **Never disparage a named competitor beyond a factual, documented comparison.**

## Testimonials and reviews

- **Real, consented, and attributable.** Consent covers the channel and the
  duration, not just the words.
- **Incentives are disclosed.** A gift, a discount, or a free product is part of
  the testimonial.
- **Never fabricate, reword to change the meaning, or quote selectively.** Cutting
  a pause is editing; reversing the sense is fabrication.
- **Never suppress negative reviews** to present a curated average as the whole
  picture.

## Price and offer

- **Price, discount, and availability are accurate at publication and stay
  current.** A stale price is a misleading claim with a checkout behind it.
- **No bait-and-switch.** The advertised item is available, in the advertised
  quantity, at the advertised price.
- **A "was" price must have been genuinely charged** for a meaningful period. A
  crossed-out number that never existed is a lie with a strikethrough.
- **No dark patterns.** No confirmshaming, no fee revealed at the last step, no
  pre-checked upsell, no cancellation maze. The friction to leave matches the
  friction to join.

## When the topic is sensitive

- **Nothing culturally insensitive, discriminatory, or politically risky ships
  without review** — by a reader from the affected group, not the team that wrote
  it.
- The absolute prohibitions on personal data and AI-generated content are in
  `deny-hard-gates`.
